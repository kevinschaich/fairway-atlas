## Audio

All recordings are from [Freesound](https://freesound.org). Shot sounds are single hits chopped from the listed recordings (several recordings contain many hits; each hit is a random alternative in game); ambience beds are trimmed, filtered, loudness-matched and loop-crossfaded excerpts. Files live in `public/audio/` (naming rules in `public/audio/README.md`); this table is regenerated from their names and `public/audio/sources.json` on every build.
